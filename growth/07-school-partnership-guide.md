## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if students don't have smartphones?**
A: Eduvy-AI works on any browser. Schools can set up shared tablets or use computer lab PCs.

**Q: Is it safe for children?**
A: Yes. All AI responses are filtered. No external links. No social features with strangers. Parent monitoring built-in.

**Q: What about students who already have tuition?**
A: Eduvy-AI complements tuition by providing instant doubt-solving and extra practice. Many students reduce tuition dependency over time.

**Q: How do teachers use it?**
A: Teachers get a dashboard showing class progress, common weak areas, and top performers. Some assign Eduvy-AI practice as homework.

**Q: What happens after the trial?**
A: Student accounts remain active on the free plan. Upgrade to preserve premium features.
